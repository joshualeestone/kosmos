# Reporting a security problem in Kosmos

If you think you have found a security problem in Kosmos, please tell us privately first. Do not
open a public issue, pull request or discussion about it.

This covers the Kosmos app, this repository, and the Kosmos websites and services, not the services
of other companies that we use.

**Email: security@installkosmos.com**

## What to include

- what the problem is, and what someone could see or do because of it
- the steps to reproduce it, as small as you can make them
- the Kosmos version you used, and your operating system
- anything else that would help us confirm it, such as a log or a screenshot, with any passwords,
  keys or other private details removed

## What happens next

We read every report and we reply to say we have it. We may ask you questions while we confirm
it. If we fix it, we tell you when the fix is out.

Please give us a reasonable chance to fix a problem before you talk about it in public.

## Please do not

- read, change or delete anyone's data but your own
- disrupt Kosmos for other people, for example by flooding it with requests
- use anything you find for more than showing us the problem

## Which versions

Fixes go into the newest release. Before you report, please check the problem is
still there in the newest version.
