# spingold-7896: the desktop loading orb holds 3:1 on light grounds (kosmos#7896 follow-up)

## Done looks like
In the light theme the desktop app's loading orb (KORB, every .spin-sweep not on a gold button) is drawn in a gold
that is 3:1 or more on every light ground, as WCAG 1.4.11 asks of a graphic; dark keeps --gold; the orb on a gold
button keeps the button's ink.

## Call made (night shift, decided)
- A token, --spin-gold: #9c741b in light, var(--gold) in system dark (hand-written) and forced dark (generated by
  tools/sync-forced-theme.js). .spin's --spin-dot reads var(--spin-gold, var(--gold)); .btn .spin's ink override is
  untouched (it sets --spin-dot itself).
- #9c741b because it is the iPhone orb's light gold (kosmos#7034's adaptive star gold, merged in #7908), so the two
  apps match, and it clears every light ground measured: page #faf9f7 4.05, card #ffffff 4.26, sunken #f4f2ee 3.81,
  side #ebe8e2 3.52.
- Rejected: --gold-deep #b88920 (2.83 sunken, 2.61 side: fails); --gold-edge #8a6614 (clears more, 4.34 to 5.26, but
  browner, and the iPhone does not use it).
- Weakest premise: the orb sits on these light grounds only. A spinner inside a dark panel while the app is light
  would get the deep gold on dark; none is known, and the check reads the page grounds only.
