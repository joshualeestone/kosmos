# #7032 part 1: the room route pages its JSON; room post words for the phone

**Done when:** GET /api/project/:id/room?n=N[&before=<at>] returns the room's last N rows (older than `before`) with
olderCount, and without ?n returns exactly what it did; GET /api/phone/notice?id=post:<post id> returns an agent's post
text (shortened) and its author, and 404 for the person's own, unknown or empty posts. Part 2 (the phone) follows in
its own PR.

## Decisions
- The cursor is a row's `at` (a time), not a position: posts arriving between two reads never shift an older page.
  Weakest premise: two rows with the exact same `at` straddling a page edge; the second would be skipped. Room rows are
  written one at a time with millisecond times, so ties are rare; reversible to an (at, id) cursor if seen.
- n reuses the text arm's 1 to 200 rule; a bad n or before is ignored, as the text arm ignores a bad n.
- The post's author comes from the post row itself, never from the id.
