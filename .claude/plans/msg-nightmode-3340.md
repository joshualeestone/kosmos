## Decisions I made (night shift — decided, not parked)
1. **Widths apply to the ROOM view (.msg), not the DM (.dm).** The DM `.dm-b` is already 92% (a
   single-column stacked thread), so "50% wider with overlap" has no meaning there and 92% cannot
   grow 50%. Josh's "agents extend right, users extend left, keep overlap" is precisely and only the
   room mechanic. Weakest premise: that screenshot 9.10.49 is the room view. Evidence: the width
   mechanic he describes exists only in the room; the DM cannot be "50% wider." Would change my mind:
   Josh saying he meant the Talk-to-agent DM.
2. **Text #fff and ground #000 scoped to the ROOM (.msg / .pjmid .thread), not the DM.** The card
   says "leave everything else the same," and he screenshotted the room. The bubble-bg TOKENS
   (--agent-msg / --usermsg-tint) are shared, so the DM bubbles get the new dark colors automatically
   and stay legible; I did NOT force the DM text/ground. If Josh wants the DM to match exactly, that
   is a one-line follow-up. Weakest premise: that he wants only the room recolored; mitigated by
   "leave everything else the same."
3. **Timestamps stay dimmer (--k-ink-2), not #fff.** "Message text" = the message body (and the
   agent name, already near-white); the timestamp is de-emphasized meta, dimmer in both themes. Not
   changing it honors "leave everything else the same."
4. **#252529 is a COOL neutral gray, not the old warm cream.** Built verbatim (Josh's exact hex).
   The two message browser-checks asserted "warm cream (R>=G>=B)" for dark; I made that arm
   theme-aware (warm cream in light, cool near-neutral dark gray in dark), keeping the strong
   distinct-from-blue guarantee.
