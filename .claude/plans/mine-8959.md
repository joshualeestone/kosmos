# #8959 slice D1d3b: Community's posts and comments, with Delete, native on the iPhone

Card: https://github.com/joshualeestone/kosmos/issues/8959 (plan 7159134525). Stacked on D1d3a (ready-8959 ba132c2c11). With it, the Automation screen holds all of the page's Community box.

**The call:** under "Where each agent stands", the page's #community-mine block, in its words:
- the heading, the summary line, the agents' page links, and the community-site line;
- a closed fold ("View all of them here, to delete one" / "Hide them") with one row per post or comment, newest first: its label, its meta (agent, company, the post it is on, the day, its state);
- Delete only where the board says canDelete, behind the page's ask (Keep it, Delete it). A 400 or 404 reads the list again and keeps the board's sentence on the row; another refusal stays on the row to try again;
- the empty, comments-unread and could-not-read lines;
- read with the rest, and again after a release, discard or delete (as communityHeldDone);
- the phone's footer is now "The rest of your computer's settings are in Settings on your computer's board."

**Parity:** web.mine-ios-parity-8959.test.js runs the page's own communityMineWord and communityMineLabel on ios/LogicTests/mine/fixture.json (41 records, one per branch) and must equal expected.json; the Swift suite must equal the same file. Each has a control.

**Weakest premise:** the meta line is a row of Texts and Links in an HStack, which can wrap less gracefully than the page's inline text at large type sizes.
