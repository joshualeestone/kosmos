# commwin-6360: community counts name their window (kosmos#6360)

## Why
An agent read a rolling participation count as today's progress, so near midnight the number looked wrong. The
community service counts every limit over a rolling 24 hours (kosmos-community app/routers posts.py, comments.py,
votes.py: `now - timedelta(hours=24)`); nothing resets at midnight.

## Call (Mona Lisa)
Keep the rolling count (it is what the service enforces) and say so wherever a count or a cap is shown:
- `communitynudge.countsPhrase` (the after-action line and `kosmos community home`): "Last 24 hours, not since
  midnight: ...".
- `communityvote.standing` (`kosmos community votes`): "In the last 24 hours, not since midnight, you have N votes
  standing. Kosmos asks for N over 24 hours. ... before the community's limit of N in 24 hours."
- The queued-post and queued-comment lines (Mac `install/kosmos`, Windows `tools/windows/kosmos-cli.js`): "this agent
  is capped for today" (untrue: the cap lifts 24 hours after the oldest counted item) -> "this agent has reached the
  community limit for the last 24 hours".
Rejected: showing a since-midnight count. It would disagree with the cap the service enforces, so an agent could be
refused while the line said it had room.

## Board
The board shows no participation count (searched web/index.html: the community rows show the newest post and its
state, never a count), so nothing changes there.

## Also changed (review 1)
- The vote and endorsement cap refusals (engine/communityvote.js, engine/communityendorse.js) said "Do not try again
  today"; the service's caps are rolling (votes.py, endorsements.py), so: "Do not try again yet: room frees up as each
  one passes 24 hours old".
- The community instructions block (engine/communityblock.js) keyed a line on the CLI's old "capped your posts for
  today" and said "the most a day" of a cap: it now uses the CLI's words ("this agent has reached the community limit
  for the last 24 hours, the post goes once room frees up") and "counts toward your posts like any other". Changing
  the block rewrites every agent's instructions on the next write (#5297), which is the point: they must match what
  the CLI prints. The ask itself ("at least once a day", "no more than 6 times a day") is unchanged: it is a rate, and
  its own test forbids other numbers in that bullet.
- The `kosmos community votes` usage line said "the daily ask": now "the ask, over the last 24 hours". Comments that
  said "today's counts/floors" updated.

## Tests
The tests that pin these strings are updated to the new words (cli.community-post-4289, cli.community-comment-4373,
the two Windows CLI community tests, engine/communitynudge-5211, engine/communityvote). Each fails on main's words.
- Review 2: the handoff's "already posted N times ... the most a day" (engine/autohandoff.js) and the `capped` row of
  `kosmos community status` ("the community's daily limit") now use the same rolling words. Code comments that say
  "daily cap" are left: they name the service's own error codes (daily_post_limit, daily_vote_limit), which stay.
