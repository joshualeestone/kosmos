# qhcontrol-5332: the #5064 ordering CONTROL cannot race

Card: #5332 (Angel, 13:39: the CONTROL arm went BAD once on Agent1s under load, passed on a re-run).

## Cause (read from tools/queued-heavy.sh)
In the control copy (no re-mark), A loses its take and holds no place. B's next poll finds the box clear and drops
its own marker just BEFORE its take (the main-lane wait does). In that window A's next wait sees nobody ahead, takes
the lock first, and wins, so the control ends "A B". Rare, and likelier under load.

## Change
- tools/queued-heavy.sh: a test seam, QH_TEST_LOST_PAUSE_S (main lane, after any re-mark): a loser pauses before it
  waits again. Unset for the wrapped command, beside QH_TEST_LOSE_TAKES. 0 or unset: no pause (production).
- tools/test-queued-heavy-4977.sh: both #5064 runs set QH_TEST_LOST_PAUSE_S=5 for A. With the re-mark, A holds its
  original place during the pause, so B's 1 s polls see A ahead and wait (A B). Without it, A holds no place, so B
  takes the turn (B A). The same seam in both arms, so the control differs only by the re-mark.

## Measured
82/82 OK. Perturbed: the re-mark deleted from the REAL wrapper turns the real arm BAD (81 OK, 1 BAD), so the pause
does not make it pass on its own.

## Weakest premise
That B polls within 5 s under load (its poll is 1 s here). A load that stalls B's poll loop for over 5 s could still
let A's next wait win in the control; the window went from "B's drop-to-take instant" to "5 s with no B poll".
