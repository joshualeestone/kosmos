# #4936: Kosmos says hello to every new team member itself (and #4935 item 6: go to the agents view)

Card: kosmos#4936 (priority, from Josh's 0.7.16 test, 2026-10-01 20:53). Branch `teamhello-4936`. Split agreed with
Mona Lisa (#4935): the finish step is mine, including #4935 item 6 (navigation); spacing, model and the making phase
are hers.

## What it does
- Once every member is seen running, Kosmos sends "hello" to each itself: the lead first, then the rest together.
  It is the request the agent's page sends (POST /api/agent/<name>/thread {text: "hello"}).
- The answer's delivery state decides the row (engine/chat.js DELIVERY): placed = "Said hello" (not "started": placed means it reached the agent; the first turn is measured live); could_not or a refusal =
  not sent, tried twice more 3 s apart (a member just seen running may still be starting), then "Could not say hello"
  with Try again; anything else (unconfirmed, no answer) = "Hello not confirmed", no retry, look at its page.
- All said hello: the step goes to the agents view (the create step's own Back destination), the new members' board cards
  (.acard/.lrow only) scrolled to, focused and marked for 10 s, and a polite live region says so. If any is not
  said hello to, the step stays and offers "Go to your team".
- While the hellos are going, and while any member is not greeted, the team counts as live, so Back or opening the team
  again resumes it with its rows and Try again. "Go to your team" is the person dismissing it.
- The hello request is one function (postWakeHello), shared with sendWakeHello.

## Decided, with reasons
- **Not autoHelloAfterRestart**: its readiness wait needs to see the agent NOT ready first, so a member already running
  would time out every time.
- **No retry of an unconfirmed hello**: chat.js says re-sending one may duplicate it; the person would see "hello"
  typed two or three times into an agent.
- **Navigate only if the person is still on the step**: Back keeps a live team running, and a screen that moves under
  someone reading another agent's chat is worse than a team found finished later.
- **Always the agents view, never a project**: the card's "or the project they came from" has no case today (the
  create view is only reached from New agent and the board's own button). What would change it: a project with its own
  "add a team" entry; then the destination follows it.
- **A test seam, TC_AUTO_HELLO**: the browser check turns it off for arms that are not about #4936, which settle on rows
  reading Running.

## Weakest premise: open, measured live on 0.7.17
That the hello itself starts the agent, not a person opening its conversation. Not measured live here: `kosmos agent
create` refuses an agent without a launch token, and the guard was not bypassed. Evidence: the page's composer sends the
identical request, and #2686's restart auto-hello wakes agents this way in production. Splinter put "make a team,
every member takes a first turn without a visit" on Josh's 0.7.17 test list; that is the live measurement.

## Decided in reviews 5 and 6
- **An unconfirmed hello does not keep the team live.** It has no retry, so it would pin the team for good and the same
  team picked again would resume a finished one. Back drops it; the row's advice was shown and the agents view marks
  the member. Rejected: keeping it live until "Go to your team" (traps the next same-kind team). Weakest premise: a
  person who leaves before reading the row loses the "look at its page" line.
- **A hello the board never answers is given up after 30 s as unconfirmed**, never re-sent.
- **Focus on the agents view is put back on the same agent's card** each time a board poll rebuilds it, for as long as
  the mark is up. After that a poll can still drop it; keeping focus across the board's rebuild in general is the
  board's own work, not this card's.
- Not changed: "Go to your agent" for a one-member team (no prebuilt team has one member).
