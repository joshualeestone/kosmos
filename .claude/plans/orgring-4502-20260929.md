# #4502: in a flat fleet, no hub line runs through another agent's face (up to what geometry allows)

## Why
Found by review it19 of #4434 (PR #4473): a flat fleet is laid out on rings, and a partly filled outer ring,
offset by its own count, put some agents at exactly the angle of an inner one. The hub's line to the outer agent
then passes through the inner face and reads as reporting to it (13 agents: 1 line; 15: 3).

## Built on PR #4473's branch (orgsector-4434), not main
#4473 rewrites orgPlace (the flat branch is unchanged there, the tree branch is new). Building on main would
conflict head-on; this branch carries #4473's commits until #4473 merges, then rebases to this card's own diff.

## Measured (scratch harness: the page's orgPlace + orgStep + orgPlanarRepair, lines counted within 22px of an
## unrelated face; reproduces the card's 13 -> 1 and 15 -> 3)
- Before: over flat fleets of 5..100, 3608 near passes at rest, in 86 of 96 sizes.
- Placement only (the second lane in the first lane's gaps): clean at first paint to 24, but at rest the springs
  and the push between faces pack the lanes (the first ring pulls in from 120px to about 92-123px), and 17, 19,
  21, 22, 23 drift back into near passes.
- Placement + the clearance force: 5..24 clean at first paint and at rest; 3161 over 5..100, in 76 sizes.

## THE CARD'S "5 TO 100" CANNOT BE MET, AND WHY (decided, not missed)
Every line runs from the hub. A face at radius r blocks a cone of about 44/r radians of directions; the first
lane (12 faces at r=120, 62px apart) blocks about 70% of the circle, and each of its 12 gaps has room for exactly
ONE line (a second in the same gap passes the first). So two full lanes, 24 agents, is the most that can be
clean; beyond that some near passes are unavoidable in any hub-and-ring layout. The change guarantees 0 up to 24
and reduces them beyond. Rejected: bigger or sparser rings (changes the most-seen chart for every fleet), and the
tree `home` pull for flat fleets (moves them onto #4473's tree physics path; a released drag would glide home).
Would change my mind: Josh wanting a different flat layout (a spiral, curved lines) for big fleets.

## Change (web/index.html)
- ORG_FACE_R = 22 (a face's radius).
- orgPlace, flat branch: lane 0 as before; lane 1 takes lane-0 gap centres, as many as it has, spread evenly;
  lanes 2+ evenly spaced and turned to the offset with the most clearance from every inner face.
- orgStep, flat fleets only: a face nearer the hub within ORG_SIM.clearReach (ORG_FACE_R + 4) of an agent's line
  pushes that agent sideways, swinging its line off (ORG_SIM.clear 0.12; 0.3 and 0.6 measured only 7% fewer
  near passes beyond 24 and push harder on the most-seen chart, so the gentle value is kept).
- Test harnesses that lift orgPlace (web.org-view, web.org-sectors-4434) add ORG_FACE_R to their constants;
  orgStep reads only ORG_SIM, so every harness that lifts the simulation keeps working.
