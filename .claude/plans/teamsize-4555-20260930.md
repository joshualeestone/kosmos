# teamsize-4555: a prebuilt team may be a lead and 1 to 5 reports

Card: kosmos#4555. Josh, 09-30 17:27 (#admin): "some of the teams were specifically supposed to be smaller.
was supposed to be like 2-6 people". Splinter aimed it at the 0.7.14 staging cut.

## The finding
`engine/catalogue.js` shapeProblem refused the WHOLE downloaded catalogue if any team was not one lead and 4
or 5 reports. So the catalogue with the packet's own team sizes (kosmos-catalogue branch teamsize-2to6) would
be refused by every board.

## The change
shapeProblem takes one lead and 1 to 5 reports. Searched for any other size rule: team create refuses only an
empty team and anything over its cap; the team screen already words "1 agent" / "N agents"; a report with no
teammates already skips the teammates line. None changed.

## Tests
`engine/catalogue.download-4632.test.js`: a lead alone (0 reports) and a lead with exactly 6 reports are
refused; a lead and 1 report is taken (asserted by the serial held, since `loaded` stays true when a download
is refused). Mutations: the old floor of 4 fails the small-team case; a ceiling of 6 fails the sixth-report
case. The 5 test files that use engine/catalogue: 427 of 427.

## Decisions
- **Call:** relax the rule to Josh's range and nothing else. **Rejected:** a per-team size field or a
  configurable range (nothing asks for it).
- **Weakest premise:** that no other code assumes a minimum team size. Searched, and reviewed twice; the
  prebuilt team picker itself is not wired yet on main, so it cannot be exercised end to end.
- **Validation:** focused, per Splinter (no new full runs on Mortals until the cut).
