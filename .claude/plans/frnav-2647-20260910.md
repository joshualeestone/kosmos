# kosmos#2647: move Check-again into the bottom nav, and fix the copy

Josh, product review 2026-09-10, from mortals. He **could not see the helper copy
at all before finishing the connection**, which is the whole reason the control
moves: the bottom nav is always visible, the mid-pane block was not.

## The four changes, as specified

1. Top copy becomes exactly: "To get the most out of your agents we need to
   ensure they can stay awake and access the computer."
2. Delete "This can take a few seconds after you flip the switch."
3. Move **[Check again]** into the bottom navigation, same area as **Next**, on
   the **far left**.
4. Keep the copy beside it, shortened to exactly: "Turned it on? Tap to check."

## 🛑 The obvious implementation is a DEAD BUTTON, and it looks correct

`.fr-acts` already has a far-left secondary slot, `#fr-alt`, driven by
`frActions(primary, alt)`. So the tempting move is to hand `#fr-alt` the
`fr-recheck` class and let the existing handler pick it up.

**It would never fire.** That handler is delegated on `#fr-pane-3`
(`document.getElementById('fr-pane-3').addEventListener('click', ...)`), and
`#fr-alt` lives in `.fr-acts`, **outside every pane**. The click never reaches
the delegate.

⭐ Nothing about that is visible from the button: it renders, it is focusable, it
styles correctly, and pressing it silently does nothing. **A moved control that
looks right and does nothing is worse than one that never moved**, because the
person concludes the check itself is broken.

⇒ The move goes through `frActions`'s `go` handler instead, which is the
mechanism that slot already has for exactly this.

## The design

- Extract the recheck body into one named function so the behaviour is not
  retyped at the new call site. **It has real logic worth not duplicating:** it
  refuses to clobber a `fr-msg-err` on the shared `#fr-s3-msg` line, both before
  and *after* the await, because a "Turn On" failure can land during it.
- Step 3 passes `alt: { label: 'Check again', go, hint }`.
- `frActions` learns `alt.hint`, rendered into a span in `.fr-acts`.
  **The hint must be CLEARED on every screen that has no hint**, or it becomes a
  sentence from screen 3 sitting under screen 5's buttons. That clearing is the
  part most likely to be forgotten, so it is asserted.
- Remove the now-dead `.fr-recheck` branch from the pane-3 delegate and the
  in-pane markup. `.fr-recheck` has exactly two occurrences (the button and the
  handler), verified, so removing both leaves nothing orphaned.

## Rejected

- **Duplicating the recheck logic at the new call site.** Two copies of the
  error-preserving rule, which is subtle and would drift.
- **A step-3-only hard-coded span in `.fr-acts`.** The nav is shared by nine
  screens; a hard-coded child is a thing every future screen has to remember to
  hide. Routing it through `frActions` means it clears itself.
- **Leaving the old in-pane button as well.** Two Check-agains is not what was
  asked, and the second would be the invisible one he complained about.

## Test plan

- A browser check on the real first-run screen: the Check-again control renders
  **in the bottom nav**, on the far left, with the hint beside it; pressing it
  actually fires a re-check (the arm that would have caught the dead-button
  design); the old in-pane control and note are **gone**.
- A negative arm: the hint does **not** appear on another screen.
- Copy asserted verbatim against Josh's exact strings, since three of the four
  items are exact wording.
- Mutation controls on every new arm.

## Weakest premise

That the bottom nav is the right home for a control that is only meaningful on
one screen. It is what Josh asked for and his reason is sound (always visible),
but it puts a screen-specific action in a shared bar, and the hint-clearing is
the maintenance cost. **What would change my mind: him saying it looks out of
place beside Next**, which is a layout call only he can make.
