## Why
Josh, #admin 2026-09-29 09:06 (verbatim on umbrella #4554): create a whole team of agents at once
(a lead plus 4 or 5 reports, good instructions, diverse AI-made human avatars) for small companies
and for personal life, and grow the 32 ready-made jobs to about 100 in categories. This card is
parts 1 and 2 (research and the seed). Team creation is #4557 (Ice Cream Kitty), the first screen
is #4556 (Angel), avatars are part 6.

## Shape (agreed with Kitty on #4557 before content was written; her four asks applied)
- `engine/catalogue-roles.js`: new roles in the existing role shape (key, group, label, blurb,
  firstAction, optional caution, instructions as lines), plus the menu group order.
- `engine/catalogue-teams.js`: teams (key, kind business|personal, rank, label, blurb, purpose,
  project {name, goal}, members). A member: slot, role key, title, suggested name, reportsTo
  (null for the lead, 'lead' for reports), focus lines, avatar spec with `image: null`.
- `engine/catalogue.js`: the one reader. `memberInstructions(teamKey, slot, names)` returns the
  full file with {{NAME}} filled (create writes explicit instructions verbatim) and an
  "On this team" section naming the lead and teammates by the names the person chose.
- Both data files are `module.exports = <JSON>;` because the Mac and Windows builds ship
  `engine/*.js` only; a `.json` file would be missing from every served build.
- Generated by `tools/catalogue/build.js` from `tools/catalogue/*-source.js` (Node, one
  toolchain; it began as Python and moved so the stale check runs everywhere); `--check` and
  engine/catalogue.test.js fail on a stale file.

## Decisions
- Leads are real menu roles, so a person can make just the lead.
- Teams reuse roles and add a title and focus; no per-team role text.
- A caution (the line shown in the picker) goes on a role whose MAIN JOB is acting for the person
  outward (sending, posting, contacting, booking, paying or refunding) or advising in a regulated
  area (money, tax, health, law, hiring). A role whose limit is incidental to its job (a CMO that
  never spends, a web developer that never touches the live site, a meal planner that never orders)
  states the limit in its own instructions only. The existing rule that fewer than half the
  catalogue carries a caution (create.test.js) is why: a caution on most roles warns about nothing.
  48 of 104 carry one (48 of the 102 on the menu, under half there too). The Habits and Sleep
  Coach, Budget Coach and School Liaison state their limits in their own text only: low stakes,
  or covered by the lead's caution on the same team. A lead carries one only when it drafts outward itself
  (the Chief of Staff, the Office Manager); the Development Director plans and reviews while its
  grant and donor reports, which draft, carry theirs. Planners whose outward step is incidental (the home maintenance planner
  finds trades, the occasions planner suggests gifts) carry none; their limit is in their own text.
- Every team carries one team-level caution for the team screen: the lead briefs and checks the
  others on its own, which no role's caution states because it comes from being a team.

## Weakest premise
The team ranking is inferred from what small businesses hand off first and what agents are most
used for; no source measures which prebuilt team people pick. Re-rank once #4557 ships.
- Leads carry no caution about briefing other agents, unlike pm. A lead's team is the agents named
  under "On this team" in its own instructions, which only team creation (#4557) writes, together
  with the team-level caution shown before the team is made. A lead made alone from the picker has
  no such section, so it does the work itself and has nobody to brief. pm differs: it can make
  agents (kosmos agent create) and briefs whoever is on the board.
- Leads do not get the pm/director oversight rhythm (read every project's tasks every fifteen
  minutes): their oversight is of their own team, which their On this team section states.
