notice-card rows #conn/#askcard/#board-msg, per-region scroll) and #pj-one-view holds the
settings/create/task/docs SUB-VIEWS; a display:contents flatten would dissolve #pj-one-view and put
those + the invariants at risk on Josh's primary view. Retune keeps the structure intact.

gaps -- this sidesteps the "nested % don't compose" problem entirely. Panel gap ZEROED so nothing
eats the fraction. Targets (all IMPLEMENTED in increment 1):

  drops the #1189 15vw+24px tuning (note in PR, intentionally superseded).
FOLD (fold-a): works BY CONSTRUCTION, NO override needed. body fold-a (3513) already sets `48px 1fr`
(Agents collapses); Projects + Right are 16.6vw fixed, so the freed Agents width flows only into the
