## Decision
Took the card's fallback (a Windows-only channel card), not the split of snapshot's per-pane closure. The closure is
~700 lines interleaving sweeps, report reconciliation and reads; a byte-identical pin on it is a big refactor for a
Windows-only outage path.

## Weakest premise
That no later step of the DM route (after the gate: trust hold, account hold, limit queue, menu hold, retry_of) needs
a card field channelCardFor leaves out. Unknown state skips the needs_you arms.
