# #4075: release.sh refuses a version that does not move forward

Found by the #4046 review (Angel): standing at 0.6.99, 0.8.00 (a whole line skipped) and 0.5.50 (backwards)
reached "no site checkout at", past every version guard. Still live on main at 0820ff9 (read tools/release.sh).

## Change (tools/release.sh, after the spelling guards, before any repo or site access)
- V compared with package.json's version as three NUMBERS (as engine/update.js compares for every install):
  - older -> refused ("older than <current>: a cut only moves forward");
  - a different major -> refused;
  - minor more than one past the current -> refused ("skips a line: the next line is 0.<n+1>");
  - not three numbers -> refused.
- Allowed: equal (a re-cut after an aborted bump, which the .99 arm already allows), any later patch in the line
  (an aborted cut can burn one), the next line at any patch.
- KOSMOS_ALLOW_VERSION_JUMP=1 lets a deliberate jump through (precedent: KOSMOS_ALLOW_STALE_TUNNEL).
- The existing spelling guards still answer first (their messages are more specific).

## Decided
- The next line at a patch other than .00 (0.6.50 -> 0.7.05) is allowed: the card asks for "V on the next line
  only"; the .00 rule belongs to the .99 arm and the spelling guard, not to this one. Weakest premise: an early
  line change mid-line may itself be a typo; the override exists for the rare deliberate case, and I did not
  refuse a legitimate-looking next-line cut.
- Rejected: `sort -V` (a second comparison that could disagree with update.js's numeric one).

## Tests (tools.release-gate.test.js, the sandbox copy that cannot touch the real repo)

  0.2.99 -> 0.3.0); the override with its control; the unparsed arm (on the 0.5 line: from 0.6 the spelling guard
  answers first). 42/42. Disabling the check: 8 fail, the controls still pass.
- Other release tests: node 58/58 (cut-home, publish-windows, win-staging, bundle.manifest, install.local-board);
  15 cut/release shell tests all pass.
