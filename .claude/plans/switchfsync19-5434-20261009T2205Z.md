# switchfsync19-5434: fourteen small setting and marker files are flushed before they count (#5434 slice 19)

Addresses #5434. Group 3 of the rename inventory: the settings switches and small markers in Kosmos's data, each saved
by writing a temp (mostly a fixed `<file>.tmp`) and renaming, with no flush. A crash could leave one at full length but
zero-filled (#5431), which reads as its default, silently undoing what the person chose (auto-update, engineering mode,
the hourly limit, the heartbeat, the recommender and assigner, the agy cap, community on/off and industry, feedback
sending, ping, tips seen, the setup-guide state, the first-run flag).

## Change
- `agycap-setting`, `assigner-setting`, `autoupdate`, `communityindustry`, `communityswitch`, `engmode`, `feedbacksend`,
  `heartbeat-setting`, `limits`, `ping`, `recommender-setting`, `tips`, `guidestate`, `firstrun`: each save calls
  `store.saveFlushed` (securewrite, atomicOnly, a unique temp, the existing mode kept). Error handling is unchanged
  (the `{ ok:false }` answers stay; tips and firstrun still throw; guidestate still swallows). `store` exports
  `saveFlushed` (the identical one-line change as slices 17 and 18).
- Stale comments about the fixed temp (limits, autoupdate, engmode) reworded.
- `engine/feedbacksend.test.js`: three tests forced a failed write by planting a directory at the old `<file>.tmp`;
  that no longer blocks the new unique temp, so they refuse the save's own `.kosmos-` temp at open instead
  (`blockSettingsWrite`), each keeping its "the write did not fail, so this proves nothing" precondition.
- New `engine/switches.fsync-5434.test.js` (7 tests), in `tools/windows-tests.js`.

## Decided
- **store.saveFlushed for all fourteen**, as slices 17 and 18. A fixed `<file>.tmp` becomes a unique temp, which also
  ends the two-process collision on it that limits.js's comment named.
- **Tests:** five driven through their public saves (engmode, limits, autoupdate, heartbeat, the first-run flag) plus
  a failed-flush arm for engmode; all fourteen held to "saveFlushed, no bare rename" by reading their source.

## Tests
- Mutation: main's fifteen files fail all 7. Related set (96 files, list5434s19.txt): 2720; one failure, create.test.js
  "the startup script, actually run, adopts a healthy agent" (spawnSync ETIMEDOUT at load 7-8), passes alone.

## Weakest premise
That no other test or guard is coupled to these fourteen files' old temp names. Grepped the tests (only
feedbacksend's three); the guards (setup-assistant, undo, backupscan) match none of them by name.
