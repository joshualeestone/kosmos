  for is gone; it now fires only for the real `.s2-allow` grant button). Updated the S2 comments.

## Decisions (night shift - decide, document, move)
- Width 205px, not a literal 1/3 (~170px). At 170px "Kosmos would like" wraps beside the folder icon,
  giving THREE lines; Josh named exactly TWO lines. His "~1/3" is approximate ("much much smaller"),
  the two-line caption is explicit, so I sized to the narrowest that fits "Kosmos would like" on line 1
  (205px, ~40% of the old ~500px -- still much smaller). Weakest premise: he may prefer a literal 1/3
  even at 3 lines; I prioritized his explicit two-line wording. One-line width tweak if he disagrees.
- Kept the macOS `"Kosmos"` process-name quotes for realism (this screen mimics a real macOS access
  dialog, which quotes the app name), consistent with #3337/#3341. Josh's card text drops the quotes;
  same weakest premise as those cards.
- The demo box's buttons are a MOCK (aria-hidden illustration). The REAL, focusable grant control is the
  `.s2-gate-row` Allow Access button -- untouched. Removing the mock buttons does not affect the grant.
