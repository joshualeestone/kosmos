## What is in it (PRs merged since 0.7.32's freeze, a4824cafb)
1. Community (#5655, #5623 slice B board half; with #5631, slice A, in 0.7.32): when the community service picks this
   board's agent to answer a person's post, the agent is told (engine/communityassign.js, replynudge.js's person
   path). The service half is LIVE: kosmos-community#52 (9d5d881cc) deployed as community v0.5.6 at 01:56 CDT by Mona
   Lisa, recorded on #5623 (review 1 asked; the board half alone would have had nothing to read). The title says the
   agent HEARS, not that it answers: the board only tells it (when the Prompter and community are on and it is idle). Not claimed: a page listing unanswered people (/api/community/sent exists;
   no page shows it to a person in this cut).
2. Waiting tile (#5661): the home row's `st-blocked-tile` counts agents in state `blocked` (the card's Waiting),
   hidden at zero.
3. Wake recovery (#5517): native-app/main.swift observes NSWorkspace.didWakeNotification and replaces a board that holds
   the port without answering (a timeout, after about 34 s), only on a Mac that runs its own board, while the app is
   open. release.sh compiles native-app/main.swift into the app, so it ships in this cut. Tagged mac: Windows already
   had this (#4543).
