# chatbox-4108: the chat boxes are at least 48px tall on a phone

kosmos#4108 (#718 re-sweep row 4, and the #4090 performance bar: Josh's Moto G Play is 360 wide), assigned by Liu Kang
(m1219). Row 7 (Open in Finder) is Sub-Zero's #4088 and is left alone.

## Measured on main (render-chatbox-phone-4108, 360x800 touch, Chromium and WebKit)
Agent Direct Message box 44px, its conversation search 44px, the project room's message box 40px, the room search
24px. Desktop (1280): 40px for the boxes, 22px for the searches.

## Done looks like
At 360x800 each of the four is at least 48px tall and neither page scrolls sideways; at 1280 each keeps 40/22; a gated
browser check proves both and fails on main; before/after screenshots at 360 on the card.

## Change (web/index.html)
- The phone block (`@media (max-width: 40rem)`): the agent composer box and the search (and its wrap) 44 -> 48, and
  `#pj-post, #pj-room-search { min-height: 48px }` for the room.
- The searches sit in a bordered `.tsearch` pill with 4px vertical padding, so a 48px input made a 58px pill (review
  1): at phone width the pill drops its vertical padding, and is the input plus its 1px border (50).
- The later `@media (hover: none)` block repeats the agent search's 44px; it comes after the phone block with the same
  specificity, so it would win at phone width: it goes to 48 too (so a touch tablet gets 48 as well).
- The room box's @-mention mirror (#pj-post-mirror) is sized to #pj-post at paint time and shares its top padding, so a
  taller box keeps the mirror on the caret.

## Rejected
- `#pj-say`: it looks like the room's box but is the one-to-one box shown only in Engineering mode (#2691, inside
  #pj-thread). The room people use is #pj-post.
- 48 at every width: desktop was not asked to change and the card pins it.

The room box's @-mention mirror was measured, not only reasoned: with "@ada hello" typed at 360 it covers #pj-post
exactly (0px off, both engines, a check arm). A touch tablet also gets the 48px agent search (the hover: none block),
a small change beyond the phone the card names. The screenshots are Chromium and WebKit; WebKit is not Safari.
