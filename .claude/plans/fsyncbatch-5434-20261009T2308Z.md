## Why one branch
The validation queue is shared; validating ten independent branches one at a time held other people's work behind
mine, so they were cherry-picked here (each slice's commits kept) and validated by one full-suite run. The only merge
conflicts were the Windows test list (every line kept) and one comment in agyhooks.js (slice 10's kept).
