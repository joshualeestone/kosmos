# #8959 slice D1d2b: Community's held posts, native on the iPhone

Card: https://github.com/joshualeestone/kosmos/issues/8959 (plan 7157930487). Stacked on D1d2a (industry-8959 43a77074a4).

**The call:** under the Community box on the phone's Automation screen, the page's #community-held list (refreshCommunityHeld, communityHeldPaint, communityHeldRelease, openCommunityDiscard), in its words:
- "Waiting for you", and the off line or the policy-stop line from the Community setting.
- Each row has its title, its words (clamped, with Read all), and its agent and state line, with every suffix the page has.
- A stopped row is named by its place, its words are hidden until "Show what it said", and it gives its reason.
- Release shows "Releasing…" and the board's refusal; a 5xx says "... The list below shows where it stands now." and reads again. Discard sits behind the page's own ask (Keep it, Discard it).
- The list's lines: unread, nothing waiting, and only the oldest 200.
- The Community hint is the page's whole line again ("then it waits for you below").

**Rejected:** a separate screen.

**Weakest premise:** a release from the phone is the same act as from the page. isViaScreen treats it so, and D1a measured that the phone passes it.

## Built
- ios/Kosmos/HeldLogic.swift; AutomationView (heldRows, heldRow, readHeld, releaseHeld, discardHeld); AutomationLogic (the hint).
- ios/LogicTests/held + run-held.sh (in ios.yml); AutomationUITests checks the sample's held and stopped posts without pressing anything that changes them.
