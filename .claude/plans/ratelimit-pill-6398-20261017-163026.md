## The mechanism for "behind the pill" (Decided)
`taskLine(a, opts)` / `stateReason(a, opts)` already take an `opts.card` flag, and the card/list
pass `{card:true}` while the **detail panel** (`#d-task`, index.html:42158) calls `taskLine(a)`
with **no** flag. The flag is already used this way at index.html:21431 (rulesBehind: "only on a
CARD"). So: suppress the rate-limited `stateReason` sentence **only when `opts.card === true`**.
Result: card/list face shows just the "Rate Limit" pill; the detail panel (behind the pill) keeps
the full sentence incl. the time and "switch its model". No new DOM, no tooltip — the least
invasive home, reusing the existing card↔detail divergence. Rejected: a `title=`/hover tooltip
(not touch-accessible, not discoverable, new markup on the pill).
