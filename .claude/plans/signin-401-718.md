# signin-401-718: a phone signed out of the relay gets a way to sign in, not "Try again"

Card: kosmos #718, Kano's phone-states list (issuecomment-5844683600), state 3. Assigned by
Liu Kang (m1082): one state per PR, a browser check that fails on main, plus a control.

## What happens today (measured)
When a device's relay sign-in has lapsed, the relay answers every script fetch with
401 and `{"signed_out":true,"error":"this device is not signed in to this Mac"}`
(kosmos-relay `crates/tunnel/src/proxy.rs` gate_page, built for exactly this: telling
"signed out" apart from "Mac not answering"). A browser NAVIGATING to the same address gets
the relay's sign-in page instead. The board ignores `signed_out`: a 401 falls into the generic
"We cannot read your agents right now ... (this device is not signed in to this Mac · status 401)"
card whose only button is "Try again", which re-fetches and gets the same 401 forever.
Kano's shot: ~/work/workers/kano/evidence/phone-states-718/st-signed-out-401--se--light--webkit.png

## Finished looks like
- A 401 whose body says `signed_out: true` draws "Sign in again to see your agents" with a
  **Sign in** button, on the agents grid/list, the org note, the projects list and the two
  project pickers, instead of the generic cannot-read card.
- The button reloads the page. A reload is a navigation, which the relay answers with its own
  sign-in page, so the person lands where they can sign in. After signing in the gate sends
  them back to the board.
- A 401 WITHOUT `signed_out` (not the relay's answer) keeps the generic card; the board's own
  403 (#2023, board token) keeps its own copy; a 500 keeps "cannot read".
- A browser check that fails on main and passes here, with those three controls, wired into
  tools/browser-checks.sh beside render-board-signin-403-2023 on the same rich board.

## Approach
- A new flag `BOARD_SIGNED_OUT`, set exactly where `BOARD_NEEDS_SIGNIN` is (the agents poll and
  loadProjects), from `status === 401 && body.signed_out === true`, and cleared by the same
  events (a readable answer, a non-answer, any other failure). Keyed on the relay's explicit
  field, not on 401 alone: a bare 401 from something else is not proof the person can fix it
  by signing in.
- Checked before BOARD_NEEDS_SIGNIN at every render site, since the two cannot both be true
  from one answer (401 vs 403) and the device state is the one with a remedy the person can do.
- Rejected: a button that links to login.kosmosplus.com. The relay's own gate page is the
  sign-in for THIS Mac's address and returns the person to it; the coordinator page does not
  know which Mac they were on.

## Wording (decided, reversible)
Headline "Sign in again to see your agents." Body: "This phone or computer was signed out of
your Kosmos. Your Mac and your agents are fine." Button "Sign in".
Liu Kang suggested "This phone needs to sign in again"; I kept the meaning but not the word
"phone", because the relay deliberately says "device" (#3837: a computer is not a phone) and a
laptop reaching the Mac remotely gets the same 401. What would change my mind: Josh or Mona
Lisa preferring the phone wording, which is a one-line change.

## Weakest part
The check stubs the relay's 401 in the page; it does not run a real relay gate. The body shape
is copied from proxy.rs on relay main (84f6566), so a relay change to that body would not be
caught here.
