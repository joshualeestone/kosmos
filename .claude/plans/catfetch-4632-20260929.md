# catfetch-4632: download the roles and teams catalogue on demand, signed, instead of shipping it

Card #4632 (Josh 2026-09-29 14:49; Splinter's call on the card). The catalogue now lives in the public
repo joshualeestone/kosmos-catalogue, which builds, signs (Ed25519) and publishes it to GitHub Pages;
installkosmos.com/catalogue/ passes through to it (chaoskosmos-site, same card, ships with the next
release). This branch is the Kosmos side.

## What changes
- engine/catalogue.js: the data is a signed copy in `<data>/catalogue/catalogue.json` (`{sig, text}`,
  one file so one rename replaces both), not bundled modules.
  - `refresh()` downloads catalogue.json and .sig when due: at most once in ten minutes, 8 s timeout,
    8 MB cap, one retry past the caches when the signature does not verify (the file and its signature
    are cached apart). Never throws; `status()` reports.
  - A copy is used only when its signature verifies against PUBLIC_KEY (the repo's
    signing-key.pub.pem), its format is 2, its serial is at least MIN_SERIAL (the first published
    serial, 1790732878) and not older than the one held, and its shape is what this module reads.
  - After a download that brings a new serial, roles.remerge() rebuilds ROLES in place.
  - With no usable copy there is no catalogue: rawRoles() and teams() are empty.
- engine/roles.js: BUILT_IN (the 35 roles in this file, rhythm included) and remerge(); the merge is
  the same as before, run at load and after each new download.
- server.js: `GET /api/roles?catalogue=1` awaits refresh() first (the role picker asks this way); every
  other caller answers from what is held. The payload carries `catalogue: status()`.
- web/index.html: the picker asks with ?catalogue=1 and asks again on the next open while the board
  holds only the built-in roles.
- Removed: engine/catalogue-roles.js, engine/catalogue-teams.js, tools/catalogue/ (moved to the repo).
- Tests: test-support/catalogue-fixture.{js,json} store the published catalogue re-signed with a test
  key in the sandboxed data root; catalogue.download-4632.test.js covers download, refusal (changed
  byte, other key, new format, no serial, below the floor), older-than-held, offline/404/oversize,
  concurrent opens, a stored copy changed on disk, the retry past caches, and drift of the built-in
  role list against the catalogue's kosmosRoles.
- CLAUDE.md: where to change a role or team now.

## Measured
- With the same catalogue stored, ROLES and all 112 member instruction files are identical to main's.
- End to end with the real embedded key: a fresh board (35 roles, no teams) refreshes from Pages and
  holds serial 1790732878, 104 roles, 21 teams.

## Calls
- Fetch on first open, not at boot: someone who only makes their own agents never downloads it.
- A signature, not a pinned hash: a catalogue edit needs no Kosmos release.
- Offline first run: the 35 built-in roles and no teams (the card's weakest premise).

## Review iteration 2 (changes)
- A signed file that is not plain UTF-8 text is refused at download (it would not survive being
  stored as text, and would vanish at the next restart); a test plants an invalid byte in a string.
- A same-serial download is written again when the stored copy is gone or no longer verifies.
- The two fetches of a pair share one abort, so one failing stops the other.
- Known worst cases, accepted: the first picker open or create for an unknown role with the network
  dead waits up to 8 s (the timeout), up to 16 s when the first answer fails its signature and the
  retry past the caches also runs; at most once in ten minutes. The picker's "Loading" covers it.
- Wording: the Team screen downloads "once #4557 lands"; CLAUDE.md says a built-in role change needs
  the catalogue repo's kosmos-builtin-roles.json and a fixture refresh.
- Tests: the em dash spellings are assembled in catalogue.test.js (no literal escape), the "not
  shipped" check has a control that scans a real folder, the browser check asserts the FIRST roles
  read is the plain one and that the list control exists, the server test asserts its sandbox.
