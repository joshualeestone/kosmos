# kosmos#8323: the day count comes off the K mark

Josh, 11-09 13:28: the number on the K mark "looks like it's a pending message that I can never get rid of".

## The call

Remove the badge, and put nothing in its place. The streak stays where it already was and cannot read as unread:
the once-a-day gold sentence out of the K mark, and the label on hover ("12 days in a row in the Kosmos"). The pill now
simply folds away.

## Rejected

- A smaller or differently coloured number on the mark. Any count on an app's icon is the shape of an unread badge.
- A new home such as the menu or Settings. Nobody asked for one, and the label already has the number on hover.

## Weakest premise

That the hover label is enough. If Josh misses seeing the count, it is one line to add somewhere else.
