# touchfields-718: the agent chat's two fields at 16px on any touchscreen

Addresses #718 (Raiden's table, row 4; Liu Kang m987).

## Finished looks like

On any touchscreen, including a phone turned sideways and a tablet, tapping the agent chat's search
box or its message box does not zoom the page (both 16px), and the search row is a 44px target.
A mouse or trackpad keeps the desktop sizes.

## Measured before (origin/main after #3882, Chromium and WebKit, touch)

- Portrait phones 375, 393, 412, 430: already fixed by #3882 (search 16px in a 44px row, text box 16px).
- 667x375, 852x393, 932x430, 1024x768: search 13px and 22px tall, text box 15px (iOS zooms on focus).

## Change

One `@media (hover: none)` block right after #3882's phone block, repeating its two field rules
(16px on `#d-talk-box .composerbox .cinput` and `#d-talk-search`, 44px search row, 42px field).
`(hover: none)` is the room's touch query (PJ_TOUCH_MQ).

## Rejected

- Widening #3882's 40rem phone block: it also switches the page to chat-first, which a tablet and a
  sideways phone do not get.
- Folding it into the DM tap-to-react PR: that is reactions, with three review rounds on it.

## Weakest part

A touchscreen laptop whose primary pointer is a trackpad reports hover and keeps 13px/15px; iOS
zoom is the reason for this rule, and no such laptop runs iOS Safari.

## Verification

render-dm-chatfirst-718 gains touch arms at 667x375, 852x393, 932x430, 1024x768 and a mouse
control at 1280 (13px/15px unchanged).
