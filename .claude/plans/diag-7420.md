# diag-7420: a diagnostic for the iPhone chat that goes black (kosmos#7420)

Josh, 2026-10-27 20:29-20:30 on build 8: the message list went BLACK when he tapped the box; bubbles shifted to the
screen edge. Neither reproduced on the simulator (iPhone 17, iOS 27, busy chat, keyboard up and down). Splinter 22:46:
reproduce, or add a diagnostic so the next occurrence tells us why. Decision on the card (comment 6460124508).
Stacked on star-7034.

## Finished looks like
- The chat keeps its last 80 scroll events in memory (numbers and event names only), and notes the keyboard's height
  and the list's size.
- When the conversation has messages but none is on screen for half a second (the black list), or the stack is wider
  than the list (the shift-left), it saves the numbers once per open chat, tries once to put the newest message back,
  and records whether that worked.
- A line in the chat: "This conversation did not draw properly." with Share what happened (the share sheet, plain
  text) and a dismiss. Nothing leaves the phone unless the person shares it.

## Decisions
- In the app only: a silent upload would need a new board route and a Mac release, and would send data without the
  person's say. Rejected.
- Weakest premise: that the black list is "no row on screen". If rows are on screen but drawn black, this does not
  fire. What would change my mind: a simulator reproduction.

## Verify
- iOS 16 typecheck (release and DEBUG); all nine LogicTests scripts (room: every trace line carries no text, name,
  draft or search words; the record is bounded; the share is the person's choice).
- Simulator: the line in light and dark (KOSMOS_SAMPLE_DIAG=1), and a busy chat with the keyboard up, backgrounded and
  resumed, where the line must not appear.
