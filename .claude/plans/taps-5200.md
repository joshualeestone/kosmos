# taps-5200: the task page's controls are 44px targets on a touch screen

Card: #5200 (found in the #5153 slice 1 design review: the shot tool's phone audit counted 10 controls under 44 px on
the task page; one was #5153's own Files toggle, fixed there). MERGE AFTER MONDAY and after Angel's #5184 (task-page
receipt), which touches the same page: rebase onto it then.

## Done looks like
- On a touch screen ((hover: none) or (pointer: coarse)), every task-page control is at least 44x44:
  Change who, Done/Put back, + Add subtask, Close this task, Add a part, Add, the message box, Send, Show me where the
  task conversations live, Due, Put on hold.
  - Boxed controls get min-height 44.
  - The two links in a row keep their look and their line, with padding and an equal negative margin. 14px above and
    below the line makes 44. Sideways each grows only AWAY from its neighbour: the name to its left, into the avatar
    gap; Done to its right. So the two never overlap, whatever the name's length.
  - The name's dashed affordance is drawn as an underline, so it stays under the name, not 14px below it.
  - + Add subtask and Close this task stand on their own lines, where a negative margin cannot be paid back exactly
    (measured: the line's strut moved the text 2 to 6px). They are honest 44px boxes instead: the subtasks block grows
    a little on a phone, and stacked boxes cannot overlap.
- Desktop with a mouse is unchanged.
- Trade-off, named: a name narrower than 25px (two letters) gets a min-width of 44, which adds a few px after it.
