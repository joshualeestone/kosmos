# grokuse-6176: a Grok subscription's usage on its account row (kosmos#6176 slice 3)

## Why
#6176 asks for usage at a glance for every provider. Claude (weekly, five-hour) and ChatGPT sign-ins (Codex's own
limits) are built and served. Left: Gemini and Grok. A read-only survey of the tools on this computer (report kept
locally) found: a Grok subscription logs its own billing reads; a Gemini subscription (Antigravity) has a usage command
that is not yet measured; an API key for either has no source of limits at all.

## Change
- `engine/groklimits.js`: reads `<account folder>/logs/unified.jsonl` (Grok's own log) from the end, 256 KB, then 1 MB,
  then 4 MB while no reading is found (measured: a busy day pushes the last reading 1.2 MB from the end). Takes the
  newest line `msg: "billing: fetched credits config"` with `ctx.config.creditUsagePercent` and
  `ctx.config.currentPeriod {start, end}`; returns codexlimits.read's shape `{ windows: [{ usedPct, windowMinutes,
  resetsAt }], plan, at }`, or null (no log, no reading, or its period is over). A line of another shape is skipped.
- `server.js` GET /api/accounts: a Grok row (provider xai) with authMode subscription gets `limits` from its own
  folder; an API-key row gets none.
- `web/index.html`: `acctLimitsUsageHtml(a, who, none)` draws a provider's own limit reports; `acctCodexUsageHtml`
  calls it as before and the new `acctGrokUsageHtml` calls it for xai rows ("Grok last reported it", and its own
  no-reading line).

## Tests
- `engine/groklimits-6176.test.js` (5): newest reading wins, an over period is dropped, nothing when nothing, a
  malformed line is skipped, a reading under 300 KB of later lines is found (red on a 256 KB-only reader).
- `server.groklimits-6176.test.js`: a subscription row carries its logged usage, one with no reading carries null, a
  key row none (red against main's server).
- `docs/browser-checks/render-account-codex-6176.js`: Grok subscription, no reading, and key rows, in Chromium and
  WebKit at 1100 and 390 (red against main's page).

## Known limits
- Only the account's own folder is read. An agent whose Grok runs from a private home of its own would log there; that
  reading is not seen (Codex's reader follows agents' private homes; Grok's does not yet).
- Grok does not log a billing read every session (none on this computer after 09-29 though Grok ran later), so a row
  can show no reading while the account is in use. The no-reading line says when Grok reports it.
- The line's shape is Grok's, not a published format; a change shows nothing rather than a wrong figure.
- Not in this slice: Gemini on a Google subscription (needs one measured run of `agy -p "/usage" --output-format
  json` first); API keys for Gemini or Grok (no source of limits; token totals are already shown, #5158).
