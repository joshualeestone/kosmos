  entry (Angel's omission, found at the 0.7.49 notes preview), so it is still unannounced to prod users and eligible.

  tablet chose Tabs for itself (the tablet's own choice, kept apart from the board's setting). Upright (portrait, 600px

  one across only on smaller, non-iPad tablets) and a project's Tasks come first; on One screen neither applies. The
  line names the sideways condition and puts the upright claim on Tabs (reviews 1 to 3), and drops "tasks first" to
  fit. #6156 said not to merge before Josh answered "split view or card grid" for landscape tablets, or noon CDT
  10-14 (stated default: split view). No answer is recorded on the PR; it merged at 17:00:23Z, noon CDT, so the
  stated default applies, and the window announces that default.
- Decision tasks say who needs you (#6157 slice B, PR #6166), rank 6: a task in the Tasks view's Needs your
  decision lane names the agent waiting on the person and the reason, in the project page's own words (PJ_NEEDS_WHY).
  A missed recurring run names no agent (it has its own repeat line), so the line says "names the agent waiting on
  you", not "which agent put it there" (review 1), and "when one is" (review 3).
- Kosmos restarts behind your app (#6158, PR #6165), rank 9, mac (titled "Updates stop stealing focus" until review
  2, then "A self-restart stays behind your app" until review 3 found "self-restart" jargon: the "Restart Kosmos to finish updating" question and the cannot-update notice can still come up over
  another app, as #6165 says): the Mac app's quiet restart onto a new version opens
  behind the app in front (unless the person pressed Restart or Kosmos was already in front). Mac only: the change is
  in native-app/main.swift.
