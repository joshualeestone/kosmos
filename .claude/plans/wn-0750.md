# wn-0750: What's New for 0.7.50

Built from the pool: `node tools/whats-new-pool.js build 0.7.50`. Prod now serves 0.7.49 (promoted 10-14 ~15:4x), so the
build recorded 0.7.49's five highlights as shown and moved lastProd from 0.7.47 to 0.7.49. Why now: the gap alarm
(kosmos#1050, 15:4x) said main is 52 commits past staging 0.7.49.

## Added to the pool
- Kosmos has its own iPad layouts (#6107, PRs #6135 and #6156), rank 3, since 0.7.49: it SHIPPED in 0.7.49 with no
  entry (Angel's omission, found at the 0.7.49 notes preview), so it is still unannounced to prod users and eligible.
  Upright (portrait, 600px to 67.99rem, touch): agent cards two across on an iPad (one across on a 7 to 8 inch tablet)
  and the project room opens with its Tasks first. Sideways (landscape, 960px and up): the Agents and Projects tabs
  open on the one-screen view (agents and projects beside the open agent or project) BY DEFAULT: a tablet's own saved
  choice wins, and an attached trackpad takes the iPad out of the touch rule (review 1 asked for "touch" and "by
  default"; review 2 found "touch iPad" confusing since every iPad is touch, and "it opens on one screen" pointing at
  the project, so the line names the view, One screen, and drops "touch": the trackpad case is rare). Upright also
  needs the board's layout on Tabs (the default): on One screen, a 13 inch iPad upright gets One screen instead. Upright, the room puts its Tasks FIRST (a sideways-swipe row above the conversation), not
  "opens on" them (review 1). "On an iPad" scopes the two across claim. Not tagged: the board's web page, whatever the computer it runs on.
- Decision tasks say who needs you (#6157 slice B, PR #6166), rank 6: a task in the Tasks view's Needs your
  decision lane names the agent waiting on the person and the reason, in the project page's own words (PJ_NEEDS_WHY).
  A missed recurring run names no agent (it has its own repeat line), so the line says "names the agent waiting on
  you", not "which agent put it there" (review 1).
- A self-restart stays behind your app (#6158, PR #6165), rank 9, mac (titled "Updates stop stealing focus" until
  review 2: the "Restart Kosmos to finish updating" question and the cannot-update notice can still come up over
  another app, as #6165 says): the Mac app's quiet restart onto a new version opens
  behind the app in front (unless the person pressed Restart or Kosmos was already in front). Mac only: the change is
  in native-app/main.swift.
