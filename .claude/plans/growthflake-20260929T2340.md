# growthflake: #4598's growth tests measure both sizes interleaved (Liu Kang m3700)

Found while looping #4560's tests: engine/orgchartfile.test.js's growth test for `<sheet` openers failed once with
"4x the input took 97.7x the time (0.05 ms -> 5.03 ms)". The helper timed three passes of the small size, then three
of the large, back to back. All three large passes landed in another agent's heavy run on the shared box, and the
best of three was still slow. It is intermittent: 1 false red in about 14 runs here. The same file on main then
passed 10 of 10 under load (load average about 12 on 12 cores), so it cannot be shown red on demand.

Change (test only): each size is warmed up, then measured in five INTERLEAVED passes (small, large, small, large...),
each the best of its five, so a busy stretch hits both sides. The wide-CSV growth test gets the same treatment. The
threshold (4x the input under 8x the time) is unchanged.

Done means: 10 runs under load pass (measured: worst 5.3x at load about 12), and the quadratic reader (994e95d09)
still fails all five growth tests (measured: 5 of 5 red).

Weakest part: interleaving makes a false red much less likely, not impossible. A busy stretch longer than the whole
measurement (about 10 x 25 ms per test) could still skew one side. The threshold's margin (8x against about 4x
measured, 16x quadratic) is what absorbs the rest.
