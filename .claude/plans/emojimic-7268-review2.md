## Findings

- [BLOCKER] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:146 (catch) with :157 (stop() early return). If `setCategory` succeeds and `setActive(true)` or `engine.start()` throws, the catch calls stop(), but request and task are still nil and the engine is not running, so stop() takes the early return: the session is left in `.record` / `.measurement` / `.duckOthers`, `savedSession` is never restored, and the input tap stays installed. Scenario: a phone call or another app holding the audio route makes `setActive` throw ("Dictation is not available right now"); from then on the app is in a record-only category, so a video or audio attachment opened later plays silent, and the other apps' audio stays ducked. Worse, on the next mic press line 117 overwrites `savedSession` with the current `.record` triple, so the original category is lost for the life of the process and no later stop() can put it back. Fix: split stop() into "cancel the session" and "release audio": always remove the tap and, when `savedSession != nil`, deactivate and restore it, even on the early-return path (or restore inside the catch before calling stop()). Also do not overwrite `savedSession` if it is already set.

- [WARNING] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:185 (announce on `.listening`). "Listening" is posted to VoiceOver at the moment the engine has just started recording with on-device recognition. On a phone without headphones VoiceOver's speech goes out the speaker straight into the open microphone, so the first words written into the box are likely "Listening" (or fragments of it). Fix: announce before `engine.start()` and delay the start until the announcement finishes (`UIAccessibility.announcementDidFinishNotification`), or drop the spoken announcement and rely on the button's label change ("Stop dictating") plus a short system-style tone, as iOS dictation does.

- [WARNING] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:216 (didSet `if state != oldValue`) with :107-112. A second press after a refusal sets the same refusal state again, so nothing changes: no VoiceOver announcement and no visible change. Scenario: speech permission denied, the person (VoiceOver user) presses the microphone again and hears nothing at all; it reads as a dead button. Fix: announce refusals from start() directly (not only from didSet), or set `state = .ready` at the top of toggle() so every refusal is a fresh transition.

- [WARNING] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift (Dictation, no interruption or scene handling). Nothing observes `AVAudioSession.interruptionNotification`, route changes, or the scene going to the background. A phone call, Siri, or the app going to the background stops the engine, but on-device recognition does not necessarily end with an error, so `state` stays `.listening`: the red stop button and "Listening" persist with a dead microphone, and `savedSession` is not restored until the person presses stop. ChatView already reacts to scenePhase for its read loop (ChatView.swift:276) but not for dictation. Fix: observe the interruption notification (and scenePhase `.background` in both composers) and call stop().

- [WARNING] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:125. `installTap` with `input.outputFormat(forBus: 0)` raises an Objective-C exception (not catchable by `do/catch`) when the format has 0 channels or 0 Hz, which happens on the Simulator without an input device and on a device whose input route is unavailable. That is a crash, not the "unavailable" line. Fix: check `format.channelCount > 0 && format.sampleRate > 0` and set `.unavailable` otherwise.

- [NIT] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:182-190 and the comment at :181 says VoiceOver hears "listening starting and stopping", but `.ready` announces nothing, so stopping is silent (including the automatic stop when someone else changes the box or recognition ends). Either announce "Stopped listening" on listening to ready, or correct the comment.

- [NIT] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:84-89 / ComposerMicButton. While a start awaits permission or setup (`starting`), the button still shows "mic" and reads "Dictate a message", yet a press cancels. Show the listening look (or a distinct "Cancel" label) whenever `active`, which needs `starting` to be @Published.

- [NIT] ios/Kosmos/ComposerExtras.swift:232. On iOS 18 the stored `selection` can be stale after dictation or Send rewrote the text (it is not cleared when `text` changes), and the bounds check compares indices taken from a different string. The emoji then goes where the old caret was, for example into the middle of freshly dictated words, rather than at the end. Not a crash in practice, but clear `selection` (or move it to the end) when `text` changes from outside, and convert through `utf16Offset`-free character offsets only after `samePosition` checks.

- [NIT] ios/Kosmos/ChatView.swift:964 and :215. The composer's onDisappear (dictation.reset) and the chat's onDisappear (draft saved to client.drafts) run in an unspecified order; a recognition result landing between them is written to draftText after the draft was saved, so the last few dictated words are lost from the per-agent draft. Stop dictation in the chat's own onDisappear before the save line.

- [NIT] ios/LogicTests/composer/main.swift. The pure rules are well covered, but the two wiring rules the change depends on are not: that both send() functions call `dictation.stop()` before the box is cleared, and that both composers wire `onChange(of: <text>) { dictation.boxChanged(to:) }`. A source-text check (as other suites in LogicTests do against web/index.html) would catch a refactor that drops either line.
