# emaildoor-529: the Gmail door says what is true today

kosmos#529 (Liu Kang m1852, from Splinter's sweep). Every Connections pill opens to a door. Everything shipped except
the email (Gmail) door, whose flow choice was left open; this card makes the call.

## Measured on main (b5c841538)
The Gmail pill (Google category) opens to its sentence plus the generic coming-soon line "When it opens up here, you
sign in on Gmail's own page, this computer holds the key, and Kosmos never sees a password": a promise of a sign-in
design nobody has chosen. The engine has no Gmail, IMAP or SMTP flow. Postmark and SendGrid (Sending email) ARE real
token doors.

## Decided
An honest coming-soon for Gmail that points at what works today: "Coming soon. Kosmos cannot connect to Gmail yet, so
no agent can read or send from your Gmail. To let an agent send email for you today, connect Postmark or SendGrid under
Sending email, further down this page." Text only. Its opening sentence (SVC_DOORS) becomes a plain "Your Google email.": the
old one ("Connect an address you choose and your agents can send and read the email you approve...") described a design
nobody chose and contradicted the line after it (review 1).

## Rejected
- A Google sign-in (OAuth) app: Josh would register it with Google, and reading mail needs Google's security review.
  That is a business commitment, not a reversible code change.
- An app password, or SMTP/IMAP login: Kosmos would hold the person's mail password, breaking the tab's promise that
  Kosmos never sees a password.
- A jump button to Sending email: web.svc-doors.test.js forbids any control in a coming-soon door (the no-fake-Connect
  rule). The rule stays; the pointer is text.

## Change
web/index.html: a SVC_SOON table (per-service honest coming-soon words, Gmail first); svcDoorText uses it, else the
generic line. Tests: web.svc-doors.test.js (Gmail's door, evaluated from the page, red on main) and
render-github-door.js (the Gmail pill opens a visible door with the text, no control).

## Weakest part
No agent can READ email today by any road. What would change my mind: Josh choosing to register a Google OAuth app,
which turns this door into a real flow.
