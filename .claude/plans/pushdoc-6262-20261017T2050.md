# pushdoc-6262: the phone push runbook says what shipped (kosmos#6262 step 7)

Owner: Ice Cream Kitty. Docs only: docs/phone-push-go-live.md.

## Why
The go-live runbook still described Step 2 with KOSMOS_PUSH=log and a later Step 8 flip. What shipped
(kosmos-relay #439, deployed 2026-10-17 20:33 CDT as relay main 56d980fe) sets the four KOSMOS_APNS_* values
in the template and never sets KOSMOS_PUSH, so APNs sends from that deploy on. A reader following the old text would
set KOSMOS_PUSH=log and silently turn push off.

## Change
Three dated notes, added above the existing text (the earlier plan is kept for the record, not rewritten):
- Step 2: what the template carries, that KOSMOS_PUSH is unset, and what a phone still needs (registered app,
  per-computer Phone notifications switch); the deploy and where its measured record is.
- Step 3b: key first (read once at startup, log-only fallback), INSTALL_ENV=1, the journal line to check, and the
  operator-only `apns-test --email` invocation under the service's user, env and sandbox.
- Step 8: not needed as written, since KOSMOS_PUSH was never set to log.

## Decided
- Notes above the old text rather than rewriting it: the old plan shows why the steps are numbered as they are, and a
  rewrite would hide that Steps 3 and 8 collapsed into one deploy.
- No local full suite: docs only, and the kosmos heavy queue runs hours deep; CI is the run (as for #6392).

## Weakest premise
Every fact in the notes is copied from the relay change and its deploy record, not re-derived. If the relay's env
template or apns-test changes, these notes go stale; they say "as shipped on 2026-10-17" so a reader can tell.
