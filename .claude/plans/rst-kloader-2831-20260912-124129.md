## What Josh asked (0.6.57 live review, 2026-09-11)

> as well, if i restart an agent from here, it should show the animated K to circle,
> send an auto hello, and tell the user to say hello to wake the agent

Three parts: (1) show the animated K-into-circle during the restart, (2) send an auto
hello, (3) tell the user to say hello to wake the agent.

## What already ships (measured, recorded on the card earlier)

- The explicit restart-from-here (`rst-go` click handler) already POSTs `/api/agent/<name>/restart`
  with the #2686-ruled partial/refused handling.
- Auto-hello already fires after a successful restart (`autoHelloAfterRestart`), #2686.
- The K-into-circle loader exists (`startKLoader`) and is already shown during a MODEL/PROVIDER
  change restart via `changeDialog({busyHtml: RESTART_BUSY_HTML})`, held for `RESTART_HOLD_MS`
  = max(2000, K_LOADER_CYCLE_MS) = 4400ms (#2692).

So the delta is narrow: the explicit `rst-go` path shows only TEXT during the restart, not the
loader. Wire the existing loader into that path, and make the say-hello nudge present on the
success receipt too (today it is only on the manual-fallback line).

## The design decision (reversible, mine to make per Josh 2026-08-31)

**Mount the branded K-loader interstitial inside the existing `rst-modal` during the in-flight
restart, held for the `RESTART_HOLD_MS` floor on success, then hand off UNCHANGED to the existing
#2686 auto-hello receipt on the page.** This reuses `RESTART_BUSY_HTML` + `startKLoader` +
`RESTART_HOLD_MS` (the same trio the model/provider interstitial uses) rather than inventing a new
visual, and it derives the hold from the loader cycle so a fast restart still plays one whole
K-into-circle animation, exactly as #2692 established for the sibling flow.

**Rejected:** routing the explicit restart through `changeDialog` itself. That flow's `run(say)`
shape and its "receipt stays in the modal" behavior conflict with the #2686 receipt SPLIT (on the
stale-instructions notice the receipt is the notice DISAPPEARING; on the detail panel it is the
`d-restart-msg` line). Reusing changeDialog would force a rewrite of that delicate, well-ruled
receipt logic for no user-visible gain. So I keep the loader as a pure interstitial and leave the
receipt mechanism exactly as it is.

### Copy

Josh wants both an auto-hello AND a say-hello nudge. #2686 deliberately made the copy say WE sent
the hello ("Restarted, and said hello to wake them.") so the person is not asked to do redundant
work. #2831 asks that the person also be told to say hello. These tension on the wording.

Resolution: keep the truthful #2686 line and append the nudge, framed so it does not contradict
"we already said hello":

- placed (success): `Restarted, and said hello to wake them. Say hello when you want to talk to them.`
- manual (fallback, unchanged): `Restarted. You will need to say 'hello' to wake them.` (already nudges)

The placed-vs-manual honesty is preserved (#2686 arm 5: never claim "said hello" unless delivery
was placed). Weakest premise: the exact reconciliation of #2686 vs #2831 on the copy is a judgment
call; it is one string and fully reversible in the app if Josh wants it shorter or worded his way.

## Steps

1. `openRestartModal`: reset the `.hidden` flags for `rst-small`, `rst-keep`, `rst-go`, and the
   static memory-cost `<p>` (give it id `rst-cost`), so every open starts from the confirm view
   even after an interstitial ran. (rst-list.hidden is already set from the cost.)
2. `rst-go` click handler: on click, capture the list's hidden state, hide the confirm content
   (rst-small, rst-list, rst-cost, rst-keep, rst-go), mount `RESTART_BUSY_HTML` into `rst-msg` and
   `startKLoader` its canvas, record `busyStart`, read the hold from
   `window.__kosmosRestartHoldMs` (test seam) else `RESTART_HOLD_MS`.
   - Refused/partial (`!restartTook`): restore the confirm view, clear the loader, write
     `restartFailureLine(out)` to `rst-msg` (literal `getElementById('rst-msg').textContent`, no
     hold) - unchanged behavior, just after tearing the loader down.
   - Success: hold for `max(0, hold - elapsed)`, then `noteFor(btn)` + `showNote('Restarted. Waking them...')`,
     clear the loader (`rst-msg.innerHTML=''`), `closeRestartModal()`, `await tick()`,
     `autoHelloAfterRestart(...)` with the new placed line + unchanged manual line.
   - Catch: restore the confirm view, clear the loader, `getElementById('rst-msg').textContent = 'We could not reach Kosmos to restart them.'`
   - Preserve the web.restart-reach.test.js contract: `noteFor(btn)` appears before
     `closeRestartModal()`; the failure/catch paths write via literal
     `getElementById('rst-msg').textContent`; the body still ends at a single trailing `\n});`.
3. `closeRestartModal`: clear `rst-msg` (detaches any mounted loader canvas so its rAF loop bails
   on `!isConnected`), mirroring changeDialog's #2692 teardown, so an Escape/backdrop dismissal
   during the hold cannot leak an animating canvas.
