# Tasks band fields take the page's field fill (unblocks the 0.7.01 re-cut)

**Branch:** `tskfields-0701` · The 0.7.01 re-cut aborted at 3b (2026-09-27 01:02 CDT)

## What happened

render-fields failed deterministically (webkit and chromium, light and dark, both attempts):
`field #tsk-by / #tsk-sort / #tsk-search is the same fill as .tsk-below`. #4095 (743711ea6, April's
Tasks restyle Josh approved on 09-26) put those fields on the `.tsk-below` band's `--k-surface` with
the same `--k-surface` fill (`select.tsk-sel`, `.tsk-search input`), leaving only a 1px `--k-rule`
border (#e3e1dc on white). render-fields' rule is absolute: a field is never the same fill as its box.
0.6.99 passed it; the PR-time browser job does not run render-fields (carded as #4119).

## Decision

One rule, scoped to the band: fields inside `.tsk-below` take the page's field fill, `--k-bg`. It
keeps #4095's layout, border and radius, and sets `background-color` only, so the selects keep their
CSS arrow gradients (which render-fields also checks).

**Rejected:** relaxing render-fields for this band (it caught a real low-contrast field), and
reverting #4095 (Josh approved the layout; only the fill fails).

## Evidence (this Mac, render-fields on the harness's rich board)

- main (4095 in): rc=1, 3 fields level with their container in each engine and scheme.
- with the rule: rc=0, 0 level, both engines, both schemes.

## Weakest premise

That `--k-bg` is the right fill for the restyle. It is the app's usual field fill on a white surface
(Mona OK'd it for the design, 01:07 CDT). The margin is thin in light mode (#faf9f7 on #ffffff is
about 1.04:1, against render-fields' 1.03 threshold), so a later warming of `--k-bg` toward white
would make the field level again; render-fields would say so.
