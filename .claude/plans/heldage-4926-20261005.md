# heldage-4926: a held room post the member already handled, or a day old, is not told

Card: #4926, reopened by the 10-05 user diagnostic (0.7.22, item R2): a session was woken on 10-05 for posts from
10-02 and 10-03 it had already handled (seen by the Grok and Gemini seats).

## Why #5001 did not cover it (read from source)
1. roomhold.withoutStale never drops an ADDRESSED held id (an @ or an answer to the member's own post), at any age.
2. A post held on the shared Google quota counts its age from the pause's end, so a long pause delivers old posts.
Which one woke the seat is not known (the wake's first line would say). The day rule counts from the post itself,
so it covers both for any post over a day old.

## Change
- messages.staleHeld: the returned Set carries `evenIfAsked`: ids the member ANSWERED (its own post in that room with
  replyTo = the id) or older than HELD_ASKED_MAX_MS (24 h, from the post itself, even after a quota pause). They are also in
  the Set. withoutStale drops evenIfAsked ids even when addressed; a judge without evenIfAsked behaves as before.
- The drop log line says why per id ("stale" or "answered or a day old").
- Every post stays in the room log; `kosmos room` shows it.

## Review 1 (Opus), BLOCKER fixed
The first version took ANY later own post in the room as handling every earlier post. A held post was never shown to
the member, so its own turn-end post (the normal case) silently dropped fresh held posts #4624 promises to tell.
Narrowed to an explicit answer (replyTo). Tested through the real idle flush: a fresh held post survives the member's
own later post; an answered ask and a day-old ask are not told. The old rule, re-applied as a mutant, turns 2 tests red.

  plain post is not recognised, so it is still told until it is a day old. The reported 10-02/10-03 posts were over a
  day old by 10-05, so the day rule covers the reported case either way.
- A day-old ask is dropped without a line to the member (logged on the board). Kept: telling it a day later is the
  wake the report complains about, and the next @ arrival's catch-up line already points to `kosmos room`.
- Wiring: the idle flush (roomhold-stale test) and the typed arrival (messages.typingbroke test, real log rows) are
  tested through the real paths; flushReleased is tested with the day rule (its own judge), and a source pin
  checks every staleHeld call in server.js passes the member.

## Review 4 (Sonnet)

  suspected paths). It now counts from the post; the 2-hour rule still counts from the pause's end. Tested both ways.
