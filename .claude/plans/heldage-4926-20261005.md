# heldage-4926: a held room post the member already handled, or a day old, is not told

Card: #4926, reopened by the 10-05 user diagnostic (0.7.22, item R2): a session was woken on 10-05 for posts from
10-02 and 10-03 it had already handled (seen by the Grok and Gemini seats).

## Why #5001 did not cover it (read from source)
1. roomhold.withoutStale never drops an ADDRESSED held id (an @ or an answer to the member's own post), at any age.
2. A post held on the shared Google quota counts its age from the pause's end, so a long pause delivers old posts.
Which one woke the seat is not known (the wake's first line would say); this covers both.

## Change
- messages.staleHeld: the returned Set carries `evenIfAsked`: ids the member HANDLED (it posted in that room after the
  post) or older than HELD_ASKED_MAX_MS (24 h, from the pause's end when there was one). They are also in the Set.
- roomhold.withoutStale drops evenIfAsked ids even when addressed. A judge without evenIfAsked behaves as before.
- Every post stays in the room log; `kosmos room` shows it. Drops are logged as before ("room-hold: ... dropped").

## Tests (engine/roomhold-stale-4926.test.js)
Handled post dropped; controls: own post before it, another member's post, own post in another room, no member named;
over/under a day; the day rule from a pause's end; withoutStale with and without evenIfAsked. Each new site perturbed:
handled rule, age rule, age-from-post, anyone-handles, withoutStale ignoring evenIfAsked; each red. Every test file
that references the hold code passes (189).

## Weakest premise
"Posted in the room since" is taken as having handled every earlier post there. A member that posted about something
else, without reading an @ to it, loses that @ from its wake (it is still in the room). Chosen because the reported
defect is the opposite one, and an @ a day old is past its moment either way.
