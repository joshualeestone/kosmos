# orglanes-4472: shrink the org chart for big teams (kosmos#4472)

Builds on #4473 (orgsector-4434, the #4434 no-crossing sectors). Branched from its head, eea0e17bf. It rebases onto main once #4473 merges.

## Done when (from the card)
- web.org-sectors-4434.test.js stays green: no crossings or overlaps.
- A lead with 30 reports and the deep 100-agent tree come back near their pre-#4434 size.
- A size test pins the numbers.
- There is a measured before/after table.

## What changed (web/index.html, orgPlace only)
1. **Two staggered rings for an all-leaf team that would crowd one ring.** The inner faces are spread evenly. Each outer face sits on the line from the manager through the midpoint between two inner faces, at ORG_LANE (= ORG_MIN_ARC) further out. clear() measures every outer line against every face on the ring (at least ORG_LANE_SLOT = 31px). Outer faces must lie inside the manager's slice. Only all-leaf teams qualify: in a team with a manager, an outer line could sweep across that manager's window.
2. **Reports ordered heaviest and lightest alternating** (orgSpread, by leaf count). Two light reports side by side set the ring radius; this was measured as the closest pair on nearly every ring.
3. **The first and last report's slice runs on to the manager's slice edges.** The slices still split the manager's slice cleanly and each face stays inside its own.
4. **Each team at its own radius** (not one radius per ring). A team is pushed out until it is clear of every face already placed, and until no line is within ORG_LINE_CLEAR (12px) of a face that is not one of its ends. The line rule is tried only up to 3x the team's first radius. A lone report's straight-out ray can be blocked by a face no radius clears (one tree reached 46,656,912px). Past that limit the team is placed by face spacing alone, as #4473 does.
5. **Test:** the turns-flat control's canvas ratio threshold goes from 2 to 1.5. That tree is now 1.8x the flat one, and its carried faces still overlap (21 pairs).

## Rejected (measured, reverted)
- **A minimum share per report:** it squeezed the heavy reports, whose windows do not grow with the radius (548,634px).
- **Slices cut from the manager's whole slice:** the median rose to 6917, and with faces at slice centres it was unbounded.
