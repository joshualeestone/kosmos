# orglanes-4472: shrink the org chart for big teams (kosmos#4472)

Builds on #4473 (orgsector-4434, the #4434 no-crossing sectors). Branched from its head, eea0e17bf. It rebases onto main once #4473 merges.

- A size test pins the numbers.

## What changed (web/index.html, orgPlace only)
1. **Two staggered rings for an all-leaf team that would crowd one ring.** Every face sits at an even half-step across the window: inner faces on the even steps, outer faces on the odd ones.
   - Each outer face goes on the line from the manager through its odd step, which is the midpoint of two inner faces. It sits ORG_LANE (= ORG_MIN_ARC) further out, pulled back to the manager's slice edge if its line would leave the slice.
   - clear() measures every outer line against every face on the ring: at least ORG_LANE_SLOT (31px) from a face's centre.
   - Only all-leaf teams qualify: in a team with a manager, an outer line could sweep across that manager's window.
   - Review it1: the first half-step layout aimed an even team's last outer face past the window, so a window bounded by the slice could never take two rings. Fixed.
2. **Reports ordered heaviest and lightest alternating** (orgSpread, by leaf count).
3. **The first and last report's slice runs on to the manager's slice edges.**
4. **Each team at its own radius**, grown in two phases:
   - first, until its faces are ORG_MIN_ARC from each other and from every face already placed, and until each branch has room for its leaves further out: `slice * (r + height * ORG_STEP) >= leaves * each * ORG_BRANCH_ROOM (0.85)`, where `each` is ORG_LANE_SLOT for an all-leaf team (it can take two rings), else ORG_MIN_ARC;
   - then, up to ORG_CLEAR_REACH (1.25x) further, until no line is within ORG_LINE_CLEAR (12px) of a face centre that is not one of its ends; if that fails, the first-phase radius stands. In the 100-agent trees for seeds 1..100, all 4,836 teams cleared at the first-phase radius.
   - Without the branch-room rule, single trees grew up to 2.4x #4473's size (review it1).
5. **Test:** the turns-flat control's canvas ratio threshold goes from 2 to 1.5. That tree is now 1.8x the flat one, and its carried faces still overlap.

- **Slices cut from the manager's whole slice:** a median of 6917, then unbounded.
- **Clearance tried from the smallest radius, up to 3x:** it blew up single trees and skipped big teams.
