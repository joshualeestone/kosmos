about 1.1:1) and lets the label identify the button, as the drawing does and as borderless buttons do elsewhere. This
is the slice's weakest premise and the first thing to undo if Josh wants the edge back: one rule.
Rejected: removing the fields' edge (a white field on the grey box would have no boundary at all); restyling .btn-quiet
(already edgeless); the danger button's edge.
