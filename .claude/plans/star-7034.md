# star-7034: star an agent from the iPhone (kosmos#7034)

Josh, 2026-10-27 20:38 CDT: "Where do I star them on the phone". Reading and sorting by stars already shipped on the
phone (starred first in the Agents list and Today); this adds SETTING a star. Stacked on sort-7421.

## Finished looks like
- Swipe right on an agent row, or long-press it, for Star / Unstar; VoiceOver has the same action. A starred row shows
  a small gold star after the name (and VoiceOver says "starred").
- The star writes the board's own PUT /api/agent-stars { name, on }, the same list the desktop's star uses.
- The agent moves at once (starred first in every order), the board's answer (the whole saved list) then decides, and a
  star that did not save goes back with the board's own words why (STAR_MSG).

## Decisions
- Swipe + long press, not a star button on every row: the row's tap opens the chat, and a second tap target on each
  row is easy to hit by mistake. Rejected: a star only in the agent's info page (two screens deep). Weakest premise: a
  swipe is not discoverable on its own; the visible gold star on starred rows and the long-press menu cover that.
  Mona Lisa may prefer a visible control; it is a small change either way.
- One save at a time per agent (a second press while one is out is ignored), as the desktop chains them.
- Colour: the board's deep gold in light (#9c741b, 3:1 on the card), its bright gold in dark.

  unstar / answer-decides through the client); server.ios-routes-6838 (the board answers the new PUT).
