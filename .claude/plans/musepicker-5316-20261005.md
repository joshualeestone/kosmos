are grey). The option WAS enabled (paintMuseOption enables it in create-provider and tc-provider once GET /api/muse

disabled. The logo list (create form, Add a provider) strips "coming soon" and shows a pill only when disabled, which
is why single-agent creation looked fine.

  installed or not signed in; was "Set up in Settings, AI Models"), else "Meta Muse · coming soon" (not switched on).
- museAsk (Add a provider) does the same for its option.
- The logo list strips " · sign in first" as it strips " · coming soon".

is genuinely not available; the first-run Meta row already follows GET /api/muse.
