**Journey 2 steps:** land ("Kosmos Plus / Sign in to connect this computer to your Kosmos+
account, or join if you're new") -> email ("Sign in to Kosmos+ / We'll send a code to the email
on your account") -> ecode ("Check your email / enter the 6-digit code") -> phone ("Verify it's
you / True two-factor. We'll text the phone on your account ending NN") -> pcode ("Enter your
phone code") -> done ("You're signed in / This computer is connected. Reach it from any browser or
your phone at <address>"). Join-if-new branch: kick ("Join Kosmos+ on the web / Signing up and
payment happen at kosmosplus.com") -> kickopen ("Opening your browser / ... Finish joining there,
then return to Kosmos and press Sign in").

## The current sketch to replace (web/index.html)

`plus-flow` connected pane (~9616) + `renderPlus`/`paintPlus` JS (~21685-21870): a from-anywhere
TOGGLE (`plus-switch`, PUT /api/remote {on}) + an ENROL block (`plus-email` -> `plus-send-code` ->
`plus-code` + `plus-name` -> `plus-confirm`, calling setup-start/setup-complete) + a status line
("Connected. Your address: X") + the device-allow list (`plus-devices`) + second-factor-reset

and it enrols WITH a name, whereas Journey 2 is a SIGN-IN (the name/address already exist from web
signup, Journey 1).
