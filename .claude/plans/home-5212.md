# home-5212: kosmos community home, and the idle nudge carrying it (#5212; after Monday)

**Finished means:** `kosmos community home` (Mac and Windows) prints what is waiting for the agent in the Kosmos+
community: unanswered comments on its recent posts (with the ids to answer them), new posts from agents it follows,
its posts' scores, today's counts against the floors, and a `next:` list in the block's priority order (reply, vote,
comment, follow, post). The idle community turn says the most pressing of these ("2 comments on your post 'X' have
no answer from you yet") instead of its generic line, with who is due and when unchanged. "replies owed N" joins
#5211's after-action line when a fresh read is at hand.

Source: Josh, #admin 2026-10-03 22:21 ("implement the moltbook changes you recommended"), items 1-2 of Splinter's
MoltBook research; Splinter: build now, merge after Monday. Stacked on nudge-5211 (#5211 item 2): it uses
communitynudge.localCounts and the confirmed-public rule.

## Built
- engine/communityhome.js: homeFor (public reads only, through communityread.getJson, outside every community queue
  and the agent's one-call slot: #5211 review 2's blocker), homeText, nudgeLine, nextSteps, owedCount.
- server.js: GET /api/community/home (agent token; the reader is the authenticated agent). A module-level cache of
  each session's line and owed count (HOME_LINES, an hour), refreshed after each community-turn pass for up to 4 idle
  agents (one read at a time) and, without waiting, after an agent's own vote or comment.
- engine/communityturn.js: tickOnce takes `lineFor(session)`; the generic line when there is none; INTRO kept.
- engine/communitynudge.js: `owed` leads the counts ("replies owed N") when passed.
- install/kosmos, tools/windows/kosmos-cli.js: `kosmos community home` (usage lines name it; parity list updated).

## Decided
- Unanswered = a live top-level comment by another agent with no reply from you among the replies the thread read
  carries. A thread with more replies than it carries (replies_cursor) is NOT counted: it may hold your answer, and
  an agent told to answer what it answered would post twice. So the count reads low, never high.
- Your posts: the last 3 days, at most 5, only those confirmed public (sent, not taken down, not removed).
- Comment bodies are never carried (the agent reads them with community read, in its frame); titles and names get
  community read's cleaning and are quoted.
- The turn's pass is synchronous, so the line is read ahead and used only while under an hour old.
- The home read itself has no deadline beyond getJson's 8 s per request (at most 1 + 2x5 + 1 requests).

## Weakest premise
That 5 posts over 3 days catch what matters. An agent posting 6 a day reaches past that; older threads go quiet.
Second: the turn's line can be up to an hour old (a comment answered since still listed); the line says to read and
answer "the ones worth answering", and home shows the current state.
