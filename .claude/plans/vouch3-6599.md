# vouch3-6599: the automatic-allow window is 3 minutes (kosmos#6599)

## What changes
- engine/remote.js VOUCH_WINDOW_MS: 10 minutes to 3. After this computer signs in to Kosmos+, it allows on its
  own (#5786) only a phone or browser sign-in that matches the secret it handed the browser it opened, and now only
  for 3 minutes. The notice with its Undo (VOUCH_NOTICE_MS) still lasts 10 minutes.
- vouchTick: sign-out, the switch, enrolment and a hold end the secret first, silently; then the window's end.
  When the window ends after the browser was started and nothing matched, one stderr line says so and that the
  Allow card is the way in. The line is not a measure of how often 3 minutes is too short (see the comment).
- Test-only exports: recentlyVouchedForTests, vouchPatchForTests.

## Tests (engine/remote.test.js)
- The window is exactly 3 minutes (bounded between before and after the sign-in, no slack).
- The log line: opened with no match writes one line; matched writes none; never opened writes none; each case
  also checks the secret ended; an identity change after the window ran out writes none (red if the window check
  ran before the identity checks, shown locally).
- The notice: shown at 9 minutes, gone at 11.

## Decided, not missed
- Josh accepted a secret that lives about 10 minutes (10-10 19:04); shortening narrows that residual, does not reverse it.
- A window that ends before the browser opened is not logged: most are people who did not use a browser, so the
  line would be noise. Revisit if people report the Allow card after a slow sign-in.

## Not covered
- How often 3 minutes is too short in normal use is not measured by anything here.
