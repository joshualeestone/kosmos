# zwnj-7892: a zero-width joiner or non-joiner inside a Persian or Indic word is spelling (kosmos#7892)

A leftover carried to #7892 from #7909's review: `shownText` escaped and marked every ZWNJ (U+200C) and ZWJ
(U+200D), so ordinary Persian and Indic names read as unusual, and a flag that fires on ordinary names stops being
read.

## Change (engine/seadmin.js)
`joinerInWord`, after IDNA's ContextJ (RFC 5892 A.1, A.2): a ZWJ or ZWNJ is kept as it is, and does not mark the
text, only where it changes how the word looks: straight after a virama, with a letter of the virama's script before
it and after the joiner (Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Sinhala);
or a ZWNJ between an Arabic letter that joins forward (Joining_Type D) and one that joins back (R or D), harakat
allowed between, by a table from ArabicShaping.txt. Anywhere else it is escaped and marked as before. Syriac and the
other joining scripts are left out on purpose (still escaped). The source carries no raw joiner.

## Review round 1 (opus)
- BLOCKER: the first rule (any joiner between two letters of one script) let through joiners that change nothing on
  screen (after an alef; a ZWJ between Arabic letters; Indic with no virama), so look-alike pairs read normal. Fixed:
  ContextJ, with each shape as a must-mark test row.
- WARNINGs: the header comment and the plan understated the gap (fixed with the rule); no test had two joining scripts
  either side (added; the reviewer's drop-same-script mutant now fails).
- NITs: a foreign virama (fixed: the virama must share the letters' script); the scripts list named as partial.

## Review round 2 (sonnet)
- WARNING: the tatweel (U+0640, Joining_Type C) and superscript alef (U+0670, T) were inside the accepted ranges, so a
  ZWNJ beside a tatweel passed. Fixed: both excluded; rows for a tatweel either side.
- WARNING: Tamil has no half forms, so a joiner after its virama changes nothing. Fixed: Tamil dropped; the comment
  says "can change" and names the font residual (a font with no conjunct shows the two alike).
- WARNING: the rows did not exercise the table. Added: dal before, a ZWNJ then a ZWJ, a virama at the start, Tamil,
  an R letter after (allowed), an Arabic-extended D (allowed). Mutants on the tatweel carve-out, Tamil and dal: red.
- NITs fixed: harakat are skipped before the ZWNJ only (comment says so); the header bullet rewrapped.

## Tests (engine/seadmin-7116.test.js)
Kept and unmarked: Persian "mi" + ZWNJ + "khaham", Hindi ka + virama + ZWJ + ssa, a ZWNJ after a harakah, Bengali
ZWNJ after its virama. Escaped and marked: Latin, two scripts, either end, Cyrillic, after an alef, ZWJ between
Arabic letters, Indic without a virama, Arabic and Devanagari either side, a foreign virama. Five mutants red.

## Weakest premise
That the Arabic Joining_Type table (R and U sets) is complete for the ranges it accepts. A letter wrongly left as D
would let a ZWNJ after it through unmarked although it changes nothing; the ranges are kept narrow for that reason.
